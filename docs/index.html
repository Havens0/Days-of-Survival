The rules are simple.

Read the story and choose how to respond. Every choice takes you to a different scene, and your actions will determine how it ends.

  • Click a response or press the matching number on your keyboard.
  • Press Space to reveal the rest of the text instantly.
  • Your current scene is saved locally on this device.
  • After an ending, you can start again and explore another route.

This is an early beta. Some story elements may change in later releases.
